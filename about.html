About Rising Edge

Engineering Precision in Every Bit and Byte

Rising Edge Technologies was founded with a singular mission: to eliminate the gap between hardware design intent and silicon reality. We believe that the majority of hardware failures are predictable — and preventable — with the right tools, training, and mindset.

Our team of hardware architects, signal integrity engineers, and embedded systems specialists works across sectors — from industrial automation and aerospace to consumer electronics and medical devices. We bring the same rigorous, physics-first approach to every engagement.

We're not just consultants. We're educators who believe that sharing knowledge makes the entire industry stronger. That philosophy drives our training platform, our open-access whitepapers, and our interactive engineering tools.

Our Values

What Drives Us

Physics First

Every design decision must be grounded in electromagnetic theory. We don't rely on rules of thumb that don't hold at high frequencies.

Simulation Before Silicon

Board spins are expensive. Our philosophy is to simulate relentlessly and iterate virtually before committing to fabrication.

Knowledge as Infrastructure

A team that understands the fundamentals builds better products. We invest in training because knowledge compounds.
