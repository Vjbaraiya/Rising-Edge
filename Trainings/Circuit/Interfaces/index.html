RS232 · RS485 · RS422
Serial Interface Hardware Design from Signal to PCB

A complete, hands-on guide to the three workhorse serial interfaces. See why a differential pair beats a single-ended line for noise and distance, compare voltage levels, cable length and data rate on live Plotly charts, wire up DE-9 and terminal blocks correctly, protect connectors against ±8 kV ESD, terminate and bias an RS485 bus, pick the right transceiver, and lay out controlled-impedance differential pairs on the board.

Introduction & Interface Comparison

Prerequisites

This course assumes basic familiarity with serial communication. You should be comfortable with:

UART / serial basics (start bits, baud rate, TX/RX)
Ohm's law, voltage dividers, and basic circuit analysis
Digital logic levels and microcontroller I/O basics
Reading component and product datasheets
