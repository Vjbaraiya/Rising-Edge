Pre-Compliance, Debug & Case Studies

A bench RI workflow with a probe/antenna and amplifier, a susceptible-band decision aid, and worked case studies.

Module progress0%

A signal generator + power amplifier driving a small antenna or near-field injection probe lets you find susceptible bands on the bench (relative, not calibrated). Always use the 80% AM modulation. The loop mirrors the Pre-Compliance methodology: illuminate → find the failing band/victim → apply one fix → re-test → step up for margin.

Safety. RF power can cause burns and interfere with other equipment — contain it (shielded enclosure/tent), respect exposure limits, and keep emissions legal.
