CE Requirements by Product Category

The standard, limit class, and dominant failure mode all shift depending on what you're building. Click through ten product categories below to see the applicable standards, typical failures, and recommended mitigation for each.

Interactive: Product Category Explorer

A single product can span multiple categories at once -- a connected medical wearable with a wireless charger might need to satisfy IEC 60601 (medical), CISPR 11 (ISM/RF), and a regional consumer standard simultaneously. Always confirm the full applicable standard set with your compliance/regulatory team before finalizing a filter design.
