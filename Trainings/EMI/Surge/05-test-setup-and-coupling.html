Test Setup, CDN & Coupling Methods

A surge test bench looks deceptively simple -- a generator, a box, some cables -- but every component exists to force the surge energy down one specific, repeatable path into the equipment under test.

Learning Objectives

🧰
Identify every component of the surge test bench and its purpose
🔀
Distinguish common-mode from differential-mode coupling
🔌
Recognize line-line, line-ground, line-neutral & neutral-ground configurations

Click each component below to see its purpose, the physics behind it, and the common variants used in real test labs.

Aspect Common-Mode Differential-Mode
Current path Both conductors to ground/earth, in phase Out and back between two conductors
Typical source Lightning-induced ground potential rise, indirect strikes Switching transients, direct line coupling
Protection component GDT or MOV line-to-ground MOV or TVS line-to-line
Relative real-world frequency More common on outdoor / long cable runs More common indoors, closer to the switching source
