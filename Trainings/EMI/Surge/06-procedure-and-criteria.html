Test Procedure & Pass/Fail Criteria

Applying the right voltage is only half the test -- how the surge is sequenced, and how the EUT's response is judged afterward, is what actually determines whether a product passes IEC 61000-4-5.

Learning Objectives

📋
Walk through the standard test procedure step by step
🔁
Understand polarity, phase-angle and repetition requirements
✅
Apply Performance Criteria A-D to judge pass/fail
Criterion Definition Result
A Continues to operate as intended, no degradation of performance below specification Pass
B Temporary loss of function or degradation which is self-recoverable Pass (if allowed by product spec)
C Temporary loss of function or degradation requiring operator intervention or system reset Pass (if allowed by product spec)
D Loss of function or degradation which is not recoverable due to damage to hardware, software, or loss of data Fail
