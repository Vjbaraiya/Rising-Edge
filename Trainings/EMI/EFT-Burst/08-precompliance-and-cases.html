Pre-Compliance, Debug & Case Studies

Bring it together: a bench burst-gen workflow, a symptom-to-fix decision aid, and worked case studies that show the debug loop end to end.

Module progress0%

A compact EFT/burst generator with a coupling clamp lets you reproduce field upsets on the bench. The workflow mirrors the Pre-Compliance debug loop: reproduce → instrument the victim → hypothesize the path → apply one fix → re-test → step up the level for margin.

Safety first. Burst generators produce kV transients — follow bonding, interlocks and PPE, and never improvise coupling on a live mains port.
